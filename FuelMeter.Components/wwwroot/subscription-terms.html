FuelMeter subscription terms

Last updated 20 November 2026.

Manual meter entry is free. An eligible account can explicitly start one 24-hour trial with one AI scan. The trial requires no payment details and expires automatically.

When available, Android subscriptions are purchased through Google Play. The purchase screen shows the price, monthly renewal frequency, included scan allowance and daily limit before confirmation. Payment and account verification must complete before paid access is granted.

Subscription access and AI scan allowances are shared across Android, Windows and the web when you sign in to the same FuelMeter account. iOS subscription access is currently disabled. Purchasing through the website or Windows app is currently unavailable.

Successful AI scans consume the allowance. Unreadable photos and failed extraction do not consume a successful-scan credit. Retry limits also apply to control repeated failures. Unused scans expire at the next subscription period. Daily limits reset at midnight UK time. Review the suggested reading before saving.

Your subscription renews automatically until cancelled. Manage or cancel through Google Play to stop the next renewal. Cancellation normally leaves access until the paid period ends, subject to refunds, revocation and Google's verified subscription status.

Deleting the app or your FuelMeter account does not itself cancel Google Play renewal. Cancel through Google Play and contact support@shrijiitservices.com if you need help.

You may have statutory cancellation and refund rights under UK law. Nothing in these terms limits those rights. For a Google Play purchase, you can request a refund through Google Play or contact FuelMeter support about an issue with the service. Google Play's purchase terms also apply.

Read the FuelMeter Privacy Policy for how account, purchase, trial and scan records are processed.
