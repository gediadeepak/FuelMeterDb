FuelMeter Privacy Policy

This policy covers the FuelMeter mobile app and web app.

Who is responsible for your data?

FuelMeter is the data controller. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact [monitored privacy email or contact form].

What information we use

  • Account information. We ask for your full name, email address and password to create an account. We store a password hash and keep account records such as your user ID and sign-in timestamps. We use your email address for account recovery. These registration details are needed to create and use a FuelMeter account.
  • Energy records and settings. If you use these features, we store the gas and electricity readings, dates, notes, supplier names, tariff details, budgets, and reminder preferences you enter or import. We use them to show your usage, estimate costs, flag unusual readings, and generate charts and statements. You can also download a copy of your data using Import / Export.
  • Meter scanning. If you take or choose a meter photo, FuelMeter sends the photo to OpenAI to suggest the fuel type and meter reading. FuelMeter stores the reading you confirm when you save it. You can enter a reading manually without sending a photo.
  • Session and device data. If you choose to stay signed in, FuelMeter stores a session token, its expiry time, and a copy of your profile in protected browser storage or device secure storage. Signing out clears that saved session. If you enable reminders on a supported device, the app uses your notification preferences to schedule them.

Why we use it and our UK lawful bases

We process account and energy data to provide the service you request, including sign-in, account recovery, and the FuelMeter features you choose to use. Our lawful basis is performance of our contract with you. We use limited account and diagnostic information where necessary to protect the service and investigate faults or abuse, based on our legitimate interests in operating a secure and reliable service. We use or disclose information where required to comply with a legal obligation.

Who receives information

If you enable Android budget alerts, FuelMeter stores your Firebase installation ID with your account and uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver budget alerts to that installation. The push message contains internal account and budget identifiers. You can turn alerts off in Notification Settings. Deleting your account removes its registered installations from FuelMeter's active database.

Our application and database hosting providers process account and usage data so they can host FuelMeter. Our email provider processes your email address, the name used in the message, and the reset message to deliver password recovery emails. OpenAI processes meter photos submitted for analysis. PDF generation can load software from a content delivery network, which receives connection details such as your IP address when your browser or app requests those files. We may disclose information to authorities where required by law.

Security, retention and deletion

We use HTTPS for FuelMeter's online service, hash account passwords, and require authentication for access to account records. Password-reset links expire after 24 hours or become invalid when used or replaced.

To delete your FuelMeter account, open Settings → Delete account in the app or visit the FuelMeter account deletion page on the web. Sign in and confirm your current password. Deletion removes your account, readings, budgets, preferences, and password-reset records from the active database.

Your UK data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask for access to your personal data, correction, erasure, restriction, or a portable copy. Use the privacy contact above to make a request.

Right to object. You can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. Make a complaint to the ICO.
