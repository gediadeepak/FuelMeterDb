Delete your FuelMeter account

Open the FuelMeter account deletion form

Sign in if prompted, enter your current password, and select Permanently delete account. In the app, the same form is available under Settings → Delete account.

If you cannot sign in, reset your password or email FuelMeter support to request deletion. Use the email address associated with your account if you can. Keep your password private.

What happens to your data?

Deletion permanently removes your active account, meter readings, budgets, settings, reminder preferences, password-reset records and registered push installations. Export any data you want to keep before deleting your account. Service logs are kept separately and may remain after deletion. Read the FuelMeter Privacy Policy for details.
